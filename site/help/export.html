Getting your files out

When your ParaAir Cloud plan ends, your drive turns read-only for 30 days so you can copy your files somewhere else. Copying them out is free. After the 30 days, the files are deleted.

When your plan ends

Your plan ends when you cancel and the time you paid for runs out, when a payment fails and isn’t fixed, after a full refund, or when free access we gave you ends. From then on, the drive is read-only for 30 days.

  • You can open files, copy them out and delete them.
  • You can’t add files or edit the ones on the drive. Anything you save to it stays on your Mac and doesn’t upload.
  • We email you when the read-only period starts, 7 days before deletion and 1 day before. Each email gives the deletion date. On a Team workspace, these emails go to the owner and every admin, and members see a notice in the ParaAir app.
  • Changed your mind? Choose a plan again in the ParaAir app before the deletion date, and the drive works as before, with nothing lost. On a Team workspace only the owner can choose a plan, so members ask the owner.

Copy everything in Finder

Your ParaAir drive is a drive in Finder like any other, so you copy from it the same way.

  1. Open the ParaAir drive in Finder.
  2. Select what you want to keep. To take everything, first press Command-Shift-Period so Finder shows hidden items, such as folders whose names start with a dot. Then choose Edit > Select All.
  3. Drag the selection to a folder on your Mac or onto another drive. Dragging to another disk copies, and the originals stay on the ParaAir drive.
  4. Leave the Mac online until the copy finishes. Then open a few of the copies to check them.

The place you copy to needs as much free space as the files take. Copying counts toward your monthly transfer, which is never charged. If this month’s full-speed transfer is used up, copying goes on at about 100 Mbit/s.

Big folders: pin, then copy

Finder fetches each file from storage as it copies. For a big folder, have ParaAir download it to your Mac first with the paraair command. If the download stops partway, run it again: it keeps what it already has. Finder then copies from your Mac’s own disk.

  1. Open Terminal. The paraair command comes inside the app. These two lines make it available in that window: 
    app="/Applications/ParaAir.app"
paraair() { "$app/Contents/Helpers/ParaAirCLI.app/Contents/MacOS/paraair" "$@"; }
    If ParaAir is in the Applications folder in your home folder, make the first line: 
    app="$HOME/Applications/ParaAir.app"
    If it’s anywhere else, Control-click ParaAir in Finder, hold Option, choose Copy “ParaAir.app” as Pathname, and paste that between the quotes on the first line.
  2. List your drives, and note your drive’s “profile” and “stateDirectory”: 
    paraair setup-status --json
  3. Pin the folder. Put in your own “stateDirectory”, “profile” and folder, written as its path on the drive starting with a slash. When the folder is on your Mac, the command prints “Pinned for offline reads”. 
    paraair --state-dir "STATE_DIRECTORY" pin PROFILE "/Projects/Film"
  4. Copy the folder in Finder, as above.
  5. Unpin it to free the space on your Mac: 
    paraair --state-dir "STATE_DIRECTORY" unpin PROFILE "/Projects/Film"

A pinned folder takes space on your Mac’s disk until you unpin it. Pinning leaves at least 5 GB of the disk free, and stops if it can’t. If you copy to the same Mac, it needs room for both copies until you unpin.

Pinning stops at a file with changes still waiting to upload. Those changes can’t upload while the drive is read-only, so copy that folder in Finder instead.

Start early

Large drives take time. Copying 1 TB takes at least 4½ hours at 500 Mbit/s, and at least 22 hours at 100 Mbit/s. Start in the first days of the 30, not after the last reminder.

You can also copy before your plan ends, when downloading is free too. Changes still waiting to upload when the plan ends can’t upload after that, so let them finish first. The ParaAir app’s home window shows how many files are waiting.

After the 30 days

From the deletion date, your files can be deleted at any time. We delete them, their recovery copies and the file index from our active systems within 30 days, and after that we can’t restore them.

Nothing on your Mac is deleted. The copies you made stay where you put them.
