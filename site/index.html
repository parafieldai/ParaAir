How it works

Big files open in pieces.

Your files live in your bucket in the cloud, cut into small pieces. Your Mac only fetches the pieces you actually use, so even a huge video opens in a moment.

Your bucketinterview.mov · 48 GB · every piece, always

Your Mac2 pieces in a small cache

Open a 48 GB video

The player’s first reads come down: the start of the file, and the index kept at its end. It opens in a moment, and the rest stays in your bucket until it’s needed.

Watch or edit as usual

The next pieces arrive just before you need them. A small cache keeps the last ones close, and when it fills, the oldest make room.

Save a change

It lands on your Mac at once. Then only the changed pieces upload, in the background, never the whole file.

No more waiting on downloads.

ParaAir streams files from your bucket straight to the app that opens them, so you never copy a whole folder down before you can start.

  • Big files open in a moment

    ParaAir fetches only the parts of a file an app reads, so a 48 GB video starts playing without downloading it first. A small cache keeps what you just used close.

  • An ordinary drive in Finder

    It sits in Finder’s sidebar like any disk. Open and save from the apps you already use, with no plugins or extensions.

  • Saves never wait on the network

    A save lands on your Mac first and uploads in the background. If the connection drops, your changes wait on your Mac and go up when it’s back.

  • Your agents work in it too

    Agents and scripts read and write the same files you do. A command line tool with JSON output lets them set up the drive and check on it.

  • Nothing gets overwritten

    When a file changed somewhere else too, ParaAir never picks a winner: keep your copy beside the original, or take the version in storage.

  • Offline when you need it

    Pin a folder with paraair pin and it downloads in full for working offline. Everything else stays in your bucket until you open it.
