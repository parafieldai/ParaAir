6.2 What does not count. These do not count toward your storage use:

  1. Recovery Copies, up to the cap in Section 10.3;
  2. the File Index and other data we keep to run the Service; and
  3. files that exist only in the local cache or write journal on your Mac and have not finished uploading.

6.3 If you reach your quota. When your storage use reaches your Plan Storage:

  1. the app tells you;
  2. new uploads pause until you free up space;

6.4 If you are over quota after a plan change. If a change to your plan leaves you storing more than your Plan Storage, your Account or Workspace becomes over quota. Uploads pause as described in Section 6.3. Nothing is deleted. You can bring your use back under your Plan Storage by deleting files.

6.5 No “unlimited”. Every ParaAir Cloud plan has a stated storage amount and a stated full-speed transfer allowance. We do not describe any plan as unlimited.

10. File recovery

10.1 What we keep. When a file is deleted, or replaced by a newer version, through the Service, we keep the old copy as a Recovery Copy for:

  1. 30 days on Personal plans; and
  2. 90 days on Team plans, once they are offered.

You can restore Recovery Copies in the ParaAir app during that window. After the window, they are deleted permanently.

10.2 Recovery Copies do not use your quota. Recovery Copies do not count toward your storage use, up to the cap below.

10.3 The cap. Recovery Copies are capped at 15% of your Plan Storage. When the cap is reached, the oldest Recovery Copies are deleted first to make room, even if their window has not ended.

Example: on a Personal plan with 1 TB, the cap is 150 GB. If you replace 400 GB of files within a few days, only the most recent 150 GB of earlier versions remain recoverable. Apps that save large files often, such as video editors with autosave, can fill the cap quickly.

10.4 Permanent deletion. You can permanently delete Recovery Copies before the window ends. On Team plans, only admins and the owner can do this. Once permanently deleted, they cannot be restored.

10.5 Limits of recovery. File recovery is not a backup. It covers only changes that reached the Service. It does not cover changes that never uploaded from your Mac, Recovery Copies deleted by the cap or by you, or files deleted when your plan ends under Section 18. If ransomware or a faulty app on your Mac damages files on the drive, the damaged versions upload like any other change. The earlier versions are recoverable only within the window and the cap.

12. Keeping your files safe: durability, backups and your part

12.1 What we do. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to keep your File Content intact and available. These include:

  1. storing File Content with Backblaze B2, which stores data redundantly across many drives;
  2. keeping the File Index in Cloudflare’s D1 database, which lets us restore it to any point in the past 30 days;
  3. keeping Recovery Copies as described in Section 10; and
  4. keeping conflicting edits from two devices as separate copies rather than silently overwriting one with the other.

12.2 What we do not promise. No storage system is perfect. We do not promise that File Content will never be lost, damaged or unavailable. In particular:

  1. we keep File Content with one storage provider in one region, and we do not keep a separate backup copy of it;
  2. the File Index’s 30-day recovery is part of the same Cloudflare service that runs it, not a separate backup;
  3. Recovery Copies are not a backup (Section 10.5); and
  4. our providers do not promise us more than their own published terms, so we cannot promise you more than that either.

12.3 What lives only on your Mac. The ParaAir app keeps a local cache, files you pin for offline use, and a write journal of changes waiting to upload. Changes in the write journal are on your Mac only until they finish uploading. The app shows which changes are still waiting. If your Mac is lost, damaged or wiped before they upload, those changes are lost. Removing the app or its data before uploads finish has the same effect.

12.4 Keep your own backup. ParaAir Cloud is a place to keep and work with your files. It should not be the only copy of anything you cannot afford to lose. We recommend keeping at least one separate copy of important files, for example on an external drive or with another storage service.

12.5 If we lose or damage your files. If File Content that finished uploading is lost or damaged because of a failure in our systems or our providers’ systems, we will:

  1. tell you without undue delay, with what we know about which files are affected;
  2. try to restore the files from any copy we or our providers hold; and
  3. refund the fees you paid for the billing period in which the loss happened.
