1. Who we are

ParaAir is offered by Parafield Inc. (“Parafield”, “we”, “us” or “our”), a Delaware corporation.

  • Postal address: 18333 Bothell Way NE, Apt 330, Bothell, WA 98011, United States
  • Email: contact@parafield.ai

For your account information and for ParaAir Cloud Personal accounts, including the files you store, Parafield is the “business” or “controller” responsible for your personal information. We use the files you store only to provide the service.

Team workspaces are not available yet. When we offer them, the organization that owns a Team workspace will be responsible for the files and other content in it, and Parafield will act as its service provider or processor (see Section 11).

2. The short version

  • If you use ParaAir with your own storage, your files never reach us. The App talks directly from your Mac to your storage provider. We do not receive your files, file names or storage credentials.
  • If you use ParaAir Cloud, we store your files so we can give them back to you. We use them only to provide the service.
  • The App sends us no analytics or telemetry.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not show ads.
  • We do not scan your files, and we do not use them to train AI models.
  • We do not routinely look at your files. Section 8 explains the limited cases where a person may access them.
  • Payments are handled by Link, Stripe’s reseller, which is the merchant of record for ParaAir Cloud. We never receive your full card number.
  • The ParaAir website uses no cookies and loads no third-party scripts.
  • You can access, export, correct and delete your information. Sections 15 and 16 explain how.

4. Two ways to use ParaAir

How we handle your information depends on how you use ParaAir.

4.1 With your own storage (free)

You can connect ParaAir to storage you already have. In this mode:

  • Your files, file names, folder structure and file metadata go directly between your Mac and your storage provider. They do not pass through Parafield’s servers.
  • Sign-in to your storage provider happens in your browser or in the App. Any access keys or sign-in tokens are stored in your Mac’s Keychain. We do not receive them.
  • The App keeps a local metadata database, a cache of file pieces, any files you pin for offline use and a journal of changes waiting to upload. All of these stay on your Mac. The App also saves backup copies of the drive’s metadata database in your own storage, so you can recover the drive.
  • The App does not send us analytics, usage data or crash reports.
  • The App checks for updates once a day by downloading a small file from the Site, and it downloads updates from GitHub. Like any web request, these requests carry your IP address and the App’s version. Cloudflare, which hosts the Site, and GitHub process them to deliver the files. We do not use them to identify or track you.
  • You do not need a ParaAir account.

Your storage provider decides how it handles your data. We have no access to your storage and cannot recover files from it for you.

4.2 With ParaAir Cloud (paid)

If you subscribe to ParaAir Cloud, Parafield stores your files and the information needed to run your drive. The rest of this policy mainly describes ParaAir Cloud.

5. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the Services. Most of it you give us directly. Some is created when you use ParaAir Cloud.

5.1 Account information

  • Your email address. It is also how you sign in.
  • Sign-in records. We sign you in with one-time codes sent to your email, so we do not store passwords. We keep only a hashed copy of each code, and codes expire after 10 minutes. After you sign in, the App keeps its sign-in tokens in your Mac’s Keychain. Our servers keep only hashed copies of them. For each signed-in session, we record when it started, when it was last used, when it expires and whether it was ended.
  • Your workspaces and drives: their names, your role and your plan.

5.2 Workspace and team information

Team workspaces are not available yet. When we offer them, we will keep:

  • the workspace name and settings;
  • the email addresses and roles (owner, admin, member or viewer) of members and invited people; and
  • invitations and when they were accepted.

If someone invites you to ParaAir Cloud or to a workspace, we receive your email address from them.

5.3 File contents (ParaAir Cloud only)

We store the files you put in your ParaAir Cloud drive, including earlier versions and recently deleted files kept for recovery. We process file contents only to store them, check their integrity, deliver them to you and anyone else with access to your workspace, and measure storage against your quota.

Your files may contain personal information about you or other people, including sensitive information. We do not ask you to store sensitive information. We treat everything you store the same way: as your content, used only to provide the service.

5.4 File metadata (ParaAir Cloud only)

To show your drive in Finder and keep it in sync, we keep metadata about your files. This includes drive, file and folder names, folder paths, sizes, permissions, created and modified times, checksums of file contents, version history, trash records, internal storage identifiers and which account made each change.

File and folder names can themselves contain personal information. We treat them as part of your content.

5.5 Usage and service records

When the App, or your browser during sign-in or checkout, talks to our servers:

  • IP address. Your IP address reaches our servers with every request, as with any internet service. We use it to answer the request and, in hashed form, to count sign-in requests so we can limit abuse. Our own logs and records do not keep IP addresses, and we do not work out your location from them. Cloudflare, which runs our servers, processes IP addresses to deliver requests and block attacks.
  • Request logs. For each request, we log the time, the kind of request, whether it succeeded, how long it took and the size of the response. Some entries also include an account, workspace or subscription identifier. Our logs never contain file names, file paths, email addresses, sign-in codes or tokens.
  • App version. The App sends its version number with each request, so we can turn away versions too old to work safely.
  • Storage and transfer. We count the storage you use and the data you upload and download in each allowance period, to apply your plan’s storage quota and monthly transfer allowance.

We use these records to run, secure and bill for the service. We do not use them to build advertising profiles.

5.6 Diagnostics

The App does not send diagnostics automatically. If you contact support, you may choose to send us logs or the output of a diagnostic command. We receive only what you send. The App’s logs record file paths only in hashed form, but other diagnostic output can include file names, so check it before you send it.

5.7 Billing information

ParaAir Cloud purchases are sold through Link, Stripe’s reseller, which is the merchant of record. Link and Stripe collect your payment details and billing address on their checkout page, take payment, calculate and collect sales tax, and issue receipts and invoices. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

We send Stripe your email address and identifiers for your workspace and your checkout, so your purchase is linked to your account. We receive from Stripe only what we need to manage your subscription: Stripe’s customer and subscription identifiers, your plan, billing period, added storage, price and currency, subscription status, free trial and renewal dates, and notices of failed payments, refunds and disputes. Stripe’s notices to our servers can include your name, billing address and card brand and last four digits; we do not store them. We never receive your full card number or security code.

5.8 Subscription consent records

Before you start a subscription, the App shows you the price, the renewal terms and how to cancel, and asks you to agree. We keep a record of that agreement: your account and workspace, the time, the plan, billing period, added storage, price and any free trial shown, the version of the disclosure text shown, the App version, and the fact that you agreed. We also use these records to give each person only one free trial.

We keep a record of each billing email we send you: which notice, the address it went to and when it was sent.

Some laws require us to keep this proof. California, for example, requires it for at least 3 years, or 1 year after the subscription ends if longer (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code 17602).

5.9 Communications

We keep messages you send us, such as support requests, feedback, privacy requests, copyright notices and counter-notices, abuse reports and legal correspondence, along with our replies.

5.10 Information from other sources

  • People who invite you to ParaAir Cloud or, when Team workspaces are offered, to a workspace give us your email address.
  • Stripe and Link send us subscription and payment status (Section 5.7).
  • People who report content may send us information about you in a copyright notice, abuse report or other complaint.
  • Government authorities may send us legal process that includes information about you.

5.11 Cookies and similar technologies

The Site uses no cookies. It does not load scripts, fonts or images from other websites, and it has no analytics or advertising tools. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes IP addresses and request details to deliver the Site and protect it from attacks.

ParaAir Cloud’s sign-in pages set one strictly necessary cookie that ties the page to your sign-in request. It expires after 15 minutes. The App stores its sign-in tokens in your Mac’s Keychain, not in cookies.

Stripe’s checkout page, the billing account page and link.com are run by Stripe and Link, which use their own cookies under their own policies.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information. We do not exchange it for money or anything else of value.
  • We do not “share” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as California law defines that term. We do not use advertising networks or show ads.
  • We do not use your files or file metadata to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, and we do not give them to anyone else for that purpose.
  • We do not scan your files. Section 8 explains.
  • We do not analyze your files to profile you, to infer your characteristics, or for any purpose other than providing the service and the limited cases in Section 8.
  • We do not collect telemetry from the App.
  • We do not access your own storage when you use ParaAir with your own storage.

8. When we look at file contents

ParaAir Cloud is not end-to-end encrypted. Your files are encrypted in transit and at rest, but the service can read them, because it has to deliver them back to you.

Our systems process file contents only to store them, compute checksums that verify their integrity, deliver them and measure storage. We do not scan, open or view the contents of your files, and we do not compare them against lists of known material (hash matching). If we ever start any kind of scanning, we will update this policy before it begins.

A Parafield staff member may access file contents or detailed file metadata only when:

  1. you ask us to, for example in a support request, and give us permission;
  2. it is needed to investigate a specific report of illegal content, a copyright notice, a violation of our Acceptable Use Policy, or a security incident;
  3. it is needed to protect someone from an emergency involving danger of death or serious physical injury; or
  4. the law requires it.

Only staff who need access for one of these reasons may have it.

If we receive a copyright notice under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act about content in your account, we may send you a copy of the notice, which can include the sender’s name and contact details. If you send a counter-notice, we must send a copy to the person who sent the original notice (17 U.S.C. 512(g)). A counter-notice includes your name, address and phone number. Our Copyright and DMCA Policy (/copyright) explains the process.

9.6 Child safety reports

If we obtain actual knowledge of apparent child sexual abuse material, or of facts or circumstances indicating child sex trafficking or enticement, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline, as 18 U.S.C. 2258A requires. A report can include account information and the content involved. NCMEC may share reports with law enforcement. We preserve the reported content for 1 year after the report, as the law requires, and we close the accounts involved.

9.7 Law enforcement and legal process

We disclose information to government authorities only when the law requires or allows it. US law, including the Stored Communications Act, limits what we can disclose:

  • basic subscriber information generally requires a subpoena;
  • other non-content records generally require a court order under 18 U.S.C. 2703(d); and
  • file contents: we require a search warrant. We do this as a matter of policy, even though the statute allows some requests for stored contents by subpoena or court order with notice to you (18 U.S.C. 2703(b)).

We may disclose information without legal process in an emergency involving danger of death or serious physical injury (18 U.S.C. 2702(b)(8) and (c)(4)). We review each request and may challenge requests that are unclear, overbroad or unlawful. Authorities outside the United States must use a mutual legal assistance treaty or another process available under US law, such as an agreement under the CLOUD Act. When we receive a preservation request under 18 U.S.C. 2703(f), we preserve the records it names for 90 days, and for another 90 days if the request is renewed. We disclose preserved records only under the legal process described above.

We will notify you before disclosing your information unless the law or a court order prohibits it, or notice would create a risk of harm or be pointless in an emergency. The ParaAir Law Enforcement Guidelines (/law-enforcement) explain more.

9.8 Protecting rights and safety

We may disclose information when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to enforce our Terms, investigate fraud or security incidents, or protect the rights, property or safety of our users, Parafield or others, as the law permits.

9.9 Business transfers

If Parafield is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that deal. The new owner must keep honoring this policy for the information it receives, or give you notice and a choice where the law requires.

9.10 With your consent

We share information in other ways only if you ask us to or agree.

9.11 Aggregated and de-identified information

We may publish or share statistics that do not identify you, such as total storage used across the service. We keep de-identified information in a form that cannot reasonably be linked to you, we do not try to re-identify it, and we require anyone we share it with to do the same.

13. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. They include:

  • encryption in transit (TLS) between the App, your browser and our servers, and encryption at rest for stored files and our database;
  • sign-in with one-time email codes, so we store no passwords, and hashed storage of sign-in codes and session tokens;
  • per-request authorization, so each request is checked against your session, your workspace and its plan;
  • storage credentials that customers never see, kept only on our servers;
  • rate limits on sign-in;
  • logs that leave out file names, file paths, email addresses and IP addresses;
  • staff access limited to those who need it; and
  • point-in-time recovery of our database for 30 days.

No system is perfectly secure. Protect the email account you sign in with, because anyone who can read your email can receive your sign-in codes, and keep your Mac secure. We take reasonable measures to protect stored files, and we also recommend that you keep your own backup of files that matter to you.

If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

15. Your rights and choices

15.1 Choices everyone has

  • Access and export your files. Your files appear as ordinary files in Finder. You can copy them out at any time. Copying files out uses your monthly transfer allowance, as the Cloud Terms (/cloud-terms) describe. If you go over the allowance, transfers slow down and we do not charge extra.
  • Update your account. To change the email address on your account, email support@parafield.ai from your current address.
  • Sign out. Signing out of the App ends that session on our servers.
  • Delete files. Deleted files stay recoverable for the recovery period (Section 12), then are permanently deleted.
  • Close your account. Email support@parafield.ai from the email address on your account. Cancel any paid plan first, so it does not renew. The ParaAir Cloud Subscription, Cancellation and Refund Policy (/subscription-policy) explains how.
  • Marketing email. We do not send any (Section 6).
  • Ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your personal information. See Section 15.2.

15.2 How to make a request

  • Email contact@parafield.ai with the subject “Privacy request”.
  • We will verify your identity by confirming the request from the email address on your account. If we cannot verify you that way, we may ask for more information. We will not ask for more than we need.
  • You can use an authorized agent. We may ask the agent for proof of your signed permission and may ask you to confirm your identity directly with us.
  • We will respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we will tell you why, and we may take up to 45 more days.
  • If we deny your request, we will explain why. You can appeal by replying to our decision with the word “Appeal”. Where possible, a person who did not make the first decision will review it, and we will answer within 45 days. If you are still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general.
  • We will not discriminate against you for using your privacy rights.

17. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

The Services do not track you over time or across other companies’ websites. We do not allow other companies to collect personal information about your online activities over time and across websites through our Site or App. Because there is no cross-site tracking to turn off, we do not change how the Services work when your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.
