Apple Silicon · macOS 26 or later

Turn on its drive

Open ParaAir and choose Open System Settings. In General, Login Items & Extensions, choose By Category, then File System Extensions, and turn on ParaAir file system.

On macOS 26, ParaAir also asks once to turn it on under App Background Activity on the same page, so it can put your drive in Finder. Choose Open Login Items in ParaAir to go there.

Make your account

In your browser, with your email address. We email you an 8‑digit code; there’s no password.

ParaAir Cloud is invite-only for now. Ask for an invite, then sign in with the address it was sent to. We email you an 8‑digit code; there’s no password.

Start your plan

You choose it on your account page once you’re signed in. Personal is $10 a month or $100 a year for 1 TB. Your first plan starts with a 7-day free trial: you give a card, and nothing is charged until the trial ends. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

Billing isn’t switched on yet, so nobody is charged. These are the planned prices; taxes may apply.

Open your drive

In ParaAir, choose Sign In to ParaAir Cloud and use the same address. Then choose your drive with Use This Drive, or make one with Create Drive. It appears in Finder, and big files open without downloading them first.
