1. The short version

  • ParaAir Cloud plans renew automatically every month or every year until you cancel.
  • Your workspace’s first subscription starts with a 7-day free trial, one per person. You give a payment card at checkout, and nothing is charged until the trial ends. If that first charge is declined, the plan ends at once.
  • Before you pay, the ParaAir app shows the price, the renewal terms, how to cancel and our refund rule, and you agree by ticking a box that is unticked by default.
  • You can cancel online at any time: in the app, on the billing account page or on link.com. You never have to call or chat with anyone.
  • You get a full refund if you ask within 14 days after your first payment, or within 14 days after any yearly renewal charge.
  • If you cancel, you keep full access until the end of the period you paid for. After that your files stay read-only for 30 days so you can download them. Then we delete them.
  • If a renewal payment fails, your plan keeps working for 14 days while the payment is retried, and we email you. We never charge late fees.
  • We email you in advance before each yearly renewal and before any price change.
  • ParaAir Cloud is sold through Link, Stripe’s reseller, which takes your payment. Parafield Inc. provides the service.

This summary is part of the policy, but the numbered sections control if anything here seems to differ.

4.2. Yearly billing. A yearly Personal plan costs less than twelve monthly payments: $100 instead of $120.

4.3. Storage. 1 TB means 1,000,000,000,000 bytes (a decimal terabyte, the same unit macOS Finder uses).

4.4. Transfer allowance. Each monthly cycle you can stream and download up to 3 times your plan’s storage at full speed. After that, transfer continues at a reduced speed of about 100 Mbit/s until the next cycle starts. We never charge for transfer and there are no overage fees. On yearly plans the cycle resets each month on the same day of the month as your billing date.

4.5. Recovery. Deleted and replaced files are kept for 30 days so you can restore them. Recovery copies do not count against your storage, but they are capped at 15% of your plan’s storage, and each one counts as at least 128 KiB toward that cap. When the cap is reached, the oldest recovery copies expire first.

4.6. No “unlimited.” No ParaAir Cloud plan offers unlimited storage or unlimited transfer, and we do not describe any plan that way.

4.7. No hidden fees. The price you see is the full price for the plan, except tax. There are no setup, activation, cancellation, late or overage fees.

4.8. Team plans are not yet available. We plan to offer a Team plan for shared workspaces. When we do, its price per member, what it includes and how member changes are billed will be shown on our pricing page and at checkout before you buy. The Team rules in sections 10.3, 12.4, 12.5, 12.7, 13.5 and 16.7 apply only once Team plans are offered.

5. Automatic renewal

5.1. Your plan renews automatically. Monthly plans renew every month. Yearly plans renew every year. Each renewal is charged at the start of the new period to your payment method on file. Renewal continues until you cancel (section 13), your payment fails and is not fixed (section 15), or we end the service (section 17).

5.2. What you are charged. Each renewal charge is your plan price plus tax. If we have told you about a price change under section 11, the new price applies only from the first renewal after the notice period.

5.3. Renewal date. Your renewal date is the same day of the month (monthly) or the same date each year (yearly) as your first payment. With a free trial, your first payment is when the trial ends, so your plan renews on that day. If that day does not exist in a given month, the renewal happens on the last day of that month. Deadlines in our emails and in the app are given as a date and time in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC).

5.4. No minimum term beyond the period you pay for. You do not commit to more than one billing period at a time. Cancelling stops the next renewal.

Each version of this wording has a version number, which we store with your agreement (section 7.4).

6.3. The summary, the full wording, the consent box and the button are all on the same screen.

6.4. After you agree, Link’s checkout page opens. Before you confirm payment, it shows the price, tax and any free trial again, together with Link’s standard payment terms.

7. How we get your consent and keep a record of it

7.1. Unticked box. To subscribe you must tick a box that is unticked by default. Its sentence states that your plan renews automatically at the stated price until you cancel, and that you agree to the Terms and this policy. You then press Start Free Trial or Continue to Payment. The button stays unavailable until you tick the box, and our server refuses to start a checkout without your agreement or when the app or account page showed an outdated version of the terms. We do not treat silence, scrolling, closing a window or continued use as consent.

7.2. Consent comes first. Link’s checkout page opens only after you agree. You enter your payment details on Link’s page, not in our app or on our website, and we do not receive your full card number.

7.3. Consent for changes. We never charge you more for a change to your subscription unless you agreed to that charge at checkout or confirm it when you make the change, with the new amount shown (section 12). We never start charging after a free trial or other free period unless you agreed to that charge at checkout (section 9).

7.4. Our records. For every agreement we keep: your account ID and workspace; the date and time; the plan, billing period, added storage and amount shown; the version of the terms shown; the app version, or that you used the account page; and Link’s checkout reference. We keep these records for at least 3 years, or 1 year after your subscription ends, whichever is longer.

It repeats the plan, the price, the date and time of your next or first charge, that the plan renews automatically, how to cancel with a direct link, our refund rule, and when you agreed and to which version of the terms.

8.2. Link also emails you a receipt for each payment. Link’s receipt does not replace our confirmation.

9. Free trial, free access and promotions

9.1. Free trial. Your workspace’s first subscription starts with a 7-day free trial. Each person gets one free trial. You give a payment card at checkout, but nothing is charged during the trial.

  • The first charge is made when the trial ends. The app and our confirmation email give its date and time, and Link’s checkout page shows the trial too. Your plan then renews on that day each month or each year.
  • If you cancel before the trial ends, you pay nothing. You keep access until the trial ends, and then section 16 applies.
  • If the first charge after the trial is declined, your plan ends at once. There is no retry period for this charge. Your files become read-only for 30 days and are then deleted (section 16). You can subscribe again during the read-only period, without a new trial.
  • If the first payment only needs you to confirm it with your bank, for example through 3-D Secure, it is not treated as declined. You get the normal 14-day period to complete or fix the payment (section 15). If the payment fails later in those 14 days, your plan still keeps working until the 14 days end.
  • The 14-day refund in section 14.1 counts from that first charge.

9.2. Promotional prices. If we offer a reduced price for a first period, the checkout states how long it lasts, the date the regular price starts and the regular price. If a free or promotional period lasts more than 31 days, we email you at least 3 and no more than 21 days before it ends, with the regular price, the date it starts and how to cancel.

9.3. No surprise conversion. We never start charging after a free period unless you agreed to that charge at checkout under section 7.

9.4. Free access by invitation. If we give you ParaAir Cloud access without a paid plan, for example during a test period, we collect no payment details and never move you to a paid plan automatically. We will email you at least 30 days before that free access ends. To keep using ParaAir Cloud after that, choose a plan at checkout under sections 6 and 7. If you do not, your files follow the read-only and deletion timeline in section 16.

10. Renewal reminders

10.1. Yearly plans. We email you between 25 and 30 days before each yearly renewal. The email states the plan, the renewal date, the amount we will charge, any price change, how to cancel online with a direct link, and that you can still get a full refund within 14 days after the renewal charge (section 14.2).

10.2. Monthly plans. Once a year, about a week before each anniversary of your first payment, we email you a reminder that your plan renews every month, with the price, what is included, the next charge date and how to cancel online.

10.3. Delivery. Reminders go to the email address on your account. For a Team workspace they go to the owner and every admin. They come from no-reply@parafield.ai as separate service emails, never as part of marketing, and they are sent even if you have opted out of marketing email. Replies to them reach us. The app also shows your renewal date, or the end of your free trial.

11. Price changes and other changes to your subscription terms

11.1. Advance notice. If we raise the price of your plan or change any other material term of your subscription, we email you at least 14 days and no more than 30 days before the first charge at the new price or the date the change takes effect. The email states the old and new price, the date the change applies and how to cancel online.

11.2. Never mid-period. A new price never applies to a period you have already paid for. It starts at your first renewal after the notice period ends. On a yearly plan that is your next yearly renewal, and the renewal reminder in section 10.1 will state the new price.

11.3. Your choice. You can cancel before the new price applies and you will not be charged it.

11.4. 14-day refund after a price increase. If you are charged a higher price and you cancel within 14 days after that charge, we refund the unused part of that period pro rata, based on the days remaining.

11.5. Price decreases apply from your next renewal without notice.

11.6. Changes to this policy. We post any change to this policy with a new effective date. A change that is material to your subscription is notified under section 11.1. Changes do not apply retroactively to charges already made.

12. Changing your plan or storage

12.1. What you choose at checkout. You choose monthly or yearly billing at checkout. Added storage is planned and not yet available. When we offer added storage, its price and terms will be shown before you buy.

12.2. Changes during a subscription are not available yet. You cannot yet change your billing period online during a subscription. To change it, cancel (section 13), and when your paid period ends, subscribe again with the billing period you want. Your files stay in ParaAir Cloud in between: they are read-only from the end of your paid period until the new plan starts, and the new plan restores full access.

12.3. When changes become available. Before we offer changes during a subscription, we will update this policy. Before any change that costs more, we will show you the amount charged now, the new recurring amount and the date it applies, and you will confirm it.

12.4. Adding team members. On a Team plan, when the workspace gains a member beyond the seats already paid for, we charge a prorated amount for the rest of the current period right away, and the workspace’s shared storage grows by 1 TB. From the next renewal, the recurring charge includes the new member. A seat still paid for this period because a member was removed (section 12.5) is not charged again.

12.5. Removing team members. On a Team plan, when a member is removed, their seat stays paid, and its storage stays in the workspace, until the end of the current period. Until then you can give that seat to someone else at no extra charge. The recurring charge drops at the next renewal. We do not refund the removed seat for the current period.

12.6. Over your storage. If you use all the storage your plan includes, nothing is deleted. New uploads stop until you free space. You can still open, download, move and delete files.

12.7. Who manages a Team subscription. The workspace owner chooses, manages and cancels the Team subscription. Billing emails go to the owner and every admin. Members cannot change billing.

13. How to cancel

13.1. Cancel online, any time. You can cancel in any of these ways:

  • In the app: open Storage Settings > Manage Plan. This opens the billing account page, where you cancel your plan.
  • On the web: open the billing account page from the link in any of our billing emails, sign in with your ParaAir account email address, and cancel your plan.
  • On link.com: sign in to link.com and cancel your ParaAir Cloud order.
  • By email: write to support@parafield.ai from your account email address, or reply to one of our billing emails. We cancel your plan and confirm by email. If a renewal charge is made after we receive your email, we refund it in full. Email is an extra option. You never need it.

13.2. What the cancellation looks like.

  • On the billing account page you cancel in a few clicks. The page shows the date your plan ends.
  • The page may ask why you are leaving. Answering is optional and never blocks cancellation.
  • We do not make offers to stay when you cancel. If we ever do, the offer will appear on the same screen as the cancel button, and you can ignore it.
  • As soon as your cancellation goes through, we email you a confirmation. It gives the date your paid access ends, the date your files become read-only and the date they can be deleted (section 16). The app shows the same dates.
  • We never require you to call, chat, talk to an agent or wait for a reply to cancel.

13.3. What cancelling does. Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep full access until the end of the period you already paid for, or the end of your free trial. After that, section 16 applies. Cancelling does not delete your files immediately and does not by itself give a refund, except as section 14 provides.

13.4. Changing your mind. Until your paid period ends, you can resume your plan on the billing account page (in the app: Storage Settings > Manage Plan) or from the link in our cancellation email. Your plan then continues on the same terms and price, with no new charge until your renewal date. After the period ends, you can subscribe again through checkout, which shows the terms and asks for your consent again.

13.5. Team. Cancelling a Team subscription ends it for the whole workspace. Removing yourself from a team is not cancellation; the team’s subscription continues.

13.6. Deleting your account. If you want your account and files deleted without waiting for the read-only period, email support@parafield.ai from your account email address. We ask you to confirm before we delete anything, because deleted files cannot be recovered. Then we cancel your subscription and delete your files from our active systems within 30 days after you confirm. Asking for deletion does not by itself give a refund, except as section 14 provides.

14. Refunds

14.1. 14-day money-back on new subscriptions. If you ask within 14 days after your first payment for a ParaAir Cloud plan, we refund that payment in full. After a free trial, your first payment is the charge made when the trial ends. You do not need to give a reason. This refund is available once per person. Refunds of yearly renewal charges under section 14.2 are not limited this way.

14.2. 14-day refund on yearly renewals. If you ask within 14 days after a yearly renewal charge, we refund that renewal charge in full.

14.3. Monthly renewals. Monthly renewal charges are not refundable after they are made, except under sections 14.4 to 14.7.

14.4. Price increases. If you cancel within 14 days after a charge at a higher price, we refund the unused part of that period pro rata (section 11.4).

14.5. Our errors. We refund in full any duplicate charge and any charge made after you cancelled. If we charge the wrong amount, we refund the difference.

14.6. If we end your service. If we stop offering ParaAir Cloud, or close your account for a reason that is not your breach of the Terms, we refund the unused part of any prepaid period pro rata (section 17).

14.7. What is not refunded, and closures for breach. Except as this policy, the Terms, the Cloud Terms or the law provides (for example Terms 15.2 and 27.3, Cloud Terms 12.5 and 16.3), we do not refund partial periods, unused storage or unused transfer allowance. If we close your account because you materially breached the Terms, the Cloud Terms or the Acceptable Use Policy (at /acceptable-use), we refund the unused part of any prepaid period, unless we closed it for fraud, child sexual abuse material or sanctions reasons, or the law requires otherwise.

14.8. How to ask. Email support@parafield.ai from your account email address, or reply to one of our billing emails. For payment and transaction questions you can also contact Link at support.link.com. We issue refunds through Link to the payment method you paid with, including the tax charged. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show the refund.

14.9. What a refund does to your plan. A full refund of your plan’s payment, under section 14.1 or 14.2 or by Link or Stripe under section 14.10, ends the plan right away. Your files then become read-only for 30 days so you can download them, and can be deleted after that (section 16). A partial refund, such as a pro-rata refund under section 14.4, 14.6 or 14.7 or a refund of the difference under section 14.5, does not by itself end your plan. A refund under Cloud Terms 12.5, or of a duplicate charge, does not end your plan. When a partial refund goes with the end of your plan, we tell you by email the date your plan ends.

14.10. Refunds by Link or Stripe. Link or Stripe may refund a purchase within 60 days in some cases, under their own terms, even where this policy would not. If they refund your plan’s payment in full, we treat the plan as ended on the refund date, and section 16 applies. A partial refund from them does not end your plan.

15. Failed payments

15.1. We tell you. If a renewal payment fails, we email you the same day and show a notice in the app. While the payment is failing, Storage Settings in the app shows Update Payment…, where you can update your payment method. You can also update it on the billing account page or on link.com.

15.2. Retry period. The payment is retried during the next 14 days. During those 14 days your plan keeps working normally. We email you again about halfway through and the day before the retry period ends. The first charge after a free trial is different (section 15.9).

15.3. If payment succeeds, your plan continues and your renewal date does not change.

15.4. If payment still fails at the end of the retry period, your subscription ends for non-payment. We email you, and your files enter the 30-day read-only period in section 16. You are not charged for the read-only period.

15.5. No late fees, no collections. We do not charge late fees or interest, and we do not send unpaid renewal amounts to collection. If a payment fails and is not fixed, your subscription simply ends.

15.6. Coming back. During the read-only period you can subscribe again through checkout, with fresh consent, and your files return to full access with nothing lost.

15.7. Card updates. Your card issuer may give Link or Stripe updated card details when your card is replaced, and they may use those details for renewals.

15.8. If Link ends the subscription early. Link may stop retrying and end the subscription before the 14 days are up. If it does, your plan still keeps working until the end of the 14 days. A new card can no longer restart that subscription. To keep full access after the 14 days, choose a plan again in the app (Storage Settings > Choose a Plan). We email you when this happens.

15.9. First charge after a free trial. The retry period does not apply to the first charge after a free trial. If that charge is declined, the plan ends at once, as section 9.1 describes. A payment that only needs you to confirm it with your bank, for example through 3-D Secure, is not a decline: sections 15.1 to 15.8 apply to it.

16. What happens to your files when a subscription ends

16.1. Timeline. Whether you cancel, a payment fails and is not fixed, or a plan ends after a full refund, the same steps follow:

StageWhenWhat you can do
Paid accessUntil the end of the period you paid for, or the end of your free trial. After a full refund, until the refund date. After a failed renewal, until the retry period ends (section 15).Everything your plan includes
Read-only30 days after paid access endsOpen, download and delete files. You cannot upload new files or change existing ones.
DeletionAfter the 30 daysFiles, recovery copies and the file index are deleted (section 16.4)

16.2. Reminders before deletion. We email you when the read-only period starts, 7 days before deletion and 1 day before deletion. For a Team workspace, we email the owner and every admin, and members see a notice in the app.

16.3. Getting your files out. Downloading your files is free during paid access and during the read-only period. You can copy them out of the ParaAir drive in Finder. The help page linked from our cancellation, read-only and deletion emails explains how. We do not charge any exit, export or switching fee.

16.4. What deletion means.

  • Our emails give the end of the read-only period as the deletion date. From that date your files can be deleted at any time.
  • We delete file contents, recovery copies and the file index (file names, folder structure, sizes and version history) from our active systems within 30 days after the read-only period ends.
  • We keep no separate backup copies of file contents. Our database provider keeps a recovery history of the file index for 30 days, so deleted index records can remain in that history for up to 30 days after deletion. We use it only to recover from a system failure.
  • After deletion we cannot restore your files.

16.5. Files on your Mac. Ending your plan does not delete anything on your Mac. Files waiting to upload and the app’s local cache stay on your Mac, also when you sign out. Changes still waiting in the app’s local upload queue cannot upload once your plan is read-only. Copy them somewhere else before your paid access ends.

16.6. When we must keep data longer. If the law requires us to keep specific data, for example under a government preservation request or another legal duty, we keep only that data, only for as long as required, and we do not use it for anything else. Billing records are kept by Link and by us for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and consent records for the period in section 7.4. The Privacy Policy (at /privacy) explains this in more detail.

16.7. Team workspaces. Workspace files belong to the workspace and are controlled by its admins, as described in the Terms. When a Team subscription ends, all workspace files follow the timeline above. When a member is removed from a team, files they added to the workspace stay in the workspace.

17. If we end the service

17.1. If we decide to stop offering ParaAir Cloud, or a plan you are on, we will give you at least 90 days’ notice by email.

17.2. We stop renewals for the affected plan and refund the unused part of any prepaid period pro rata. The service works normally for the first 60 days of the notice period and is read-only for the last 30 days. Your files are then deleted as section 16.4 describes. Section 17 of the Cloud Terms gives more detail, including the rare emergencies in which we cannot give this much notice.

17.3. If we close your account for a reason that is not your breach of the Terms, the same notice, refund and download rights apply.

18.2. You are charged in US dollars.

18.3. Link emails you a receipt for every payment. You can also see your past invoices on the billing account page, and your order on link.com.

19. Disputes about a charge

19.1. If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us first at support@parafield.ai. We aim to reply within 2 business days, and we fix billing errors quickly (section 14.5).

19.2. If you dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer, Link handles the dispute with them. While the dispute is open, your account is under review and your workspace is read-only: you can open, download and delete files, but not upload new ones. Renewals continue while the dispute is open, unless we pause them. We do not delete files because of a dispute. If the dispute is closed in our favor, full access returns, unless your plan has ended for another reason. If it is closed in your favor, we review your account and email you about your plan. If your plan ends, the 30-day read-only period in section 16 still applies before any deletion.

19.3. Other disputes are governed by the dispute-resolution section of the Terms (at /terms).
