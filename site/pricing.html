Questions.

Which storage can I bring?

Cloudflare R2 works today, and it’s the one we’ve tested. The app also connects to Backblaze B2, Amazon S3 and a NAS that serves an S3 API over HTTPS. With your own storage, the drive is for one Mac at a time.

Do the agent features work on my own bucket?

No. The change feed, file versions, conflict copies, folder permissions and service credentials need ParaAir Cloud. On your own bucket, ParaAir is a drive for one Mac.

What isn’t ParaAir for?

Folders that need a full Mac disk. ParaAir doesn’t keep symlinks, hard links or extended attributes, so don’t use it for Photos libraries, Git working copies, databases or Time Machine. Finder tags don’t sync.

What if I stop using ParaAir?

The app is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence. On your own storage, your files stay in your bucket in JuiceFS’s open format, not one of ours. On ParaAir Cloud, if you cancel you keep full access until the end of the period you paid for, or your trial. After that your files stay read-only for 30 days so you can copy them out.

What counts as transfer?

Streaming and downloads: the data read from your ParaAir Cloud storage. Each month includes 3× your plan’s storage at full speed, which is 3 TB on Personal.

What happens at the limit?

Speed drops to about 100 Mbit/s until the next cycle, then full speed returns. There are no overage charges.

How does the free trial work?

Your first plan starts with 7 free days, one trial per person. You give a card at checkout and nothing is charged until the trial ends. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. If that first charge is declined, the plan ends at once.

What does cancelling do?

Your plan stops renewing. You keep full access to the end of the period you paid for, or of your free trial. Then your files are read-only for 30 days, so you can still open and download them, and after that we delete them. You cancel on the billing account page: choose Manage Plan in the app’s Storage Settings, or follow the link in any billing email. The subscription policy covers renewals, cancelling and refunds.
