Transfer, limits and cancelling.

What counts as transfer?

Streaming and downloads: the data read from your ParaAir Cloud storage. Each month includes 3× your plan’s storage at full speed, which is 3 TB on Personal.

What happens at the limit?

Speed drops to about 100 Mbit/s until the next cycle, then full speed returns. There are no overage charges.

What does cancelling do?

You keep read-only access for 30 days, so you can still open and copy your files.
